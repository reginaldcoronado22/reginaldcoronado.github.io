01 // About & Editorial Approach

Story-First Post-Production Built for Modern Attention Spans

I am Reginald G. Coronado, a multidisciplinary video editor, motion designer, and digital content creator dedicated to shaping raw camera footage into cohesive, emotionally resonant stories. Every cut, speed ramp, and sound transition in my timeline serves a single purpose: holding viewer momentum from the opening frame to the final call to action.

Through hands-on production for my YouTube channel @UtoysTvmagz as well as commercial and social media projects, I manage the complete post-production pipeline—spanning narrative assembly, multi-cam synchronization, custom kinetic lower-thirds, Rec.709 color grading, and broadcast-ready loudness normalization.

To give clients and creative directors an interactive look at how I think about pacing and finishing, I also built Corona Cut—a browser-native 30-second video stitcher where you can test transitions, color LUTs, and timeline structure firsthand.
